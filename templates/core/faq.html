Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers regarding our books, order processing, delivery timelines, and return policies.

Are all the books sold on Sonu Book Store genuine?

Yes, 100%. We source all our books directly from certified publishers and authorized distributors, ensuring authentic print quality and editions.

How long does shipping and delivery take?

Orders are dispatched within 24 to 48 hours. Delivery usually takes 3–5 business days depending on your location, with tracking numbers shared via SMS and email.

How are the books packaged to prevent damage?

We use multi-layer protective packaging, including bubble wrap and waterproof outer mailers, ensuring your books arrive in mint condition.

What is your return or replacement policy?

If you receive a damaged book or the wrong edition, contact our support within 7 days of delivery. We will initiate a replacement or refund immediately.

Which payment methods are accepted?

We support all major payment options, including UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm), Debit/Credit Cards, Net Banking, and Cash on Delivery (COD) on eligible pin codes.
