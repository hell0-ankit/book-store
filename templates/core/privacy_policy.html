Privacy Policy

At Sonu Book Store, we value your trust and are committed to protecting your personal information.

1. Information We Collect

When you place an order or browse Sonu Book Store, we collect standard account and transaction details, including:

  • Contact details: Name, email address, phone number, and delivery addresses.
  • Order data: Books purchased, order dates, and shipping tracking information.
  • Technical logs: IP address, device type, and browser preferences to improve site performance.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is processed strictly for fulfilling our service to you:

  • Processing, packaging, and delivering your book orders accurately.
  • Sending order confirmations, tracking alerts, and invoice details.
  • Providing customer assistance and handling return/replacement requests.

3. Payment & Third-Party Security

We do not store your debit card, credit card, or UPI credentials. All transactions are securely routed through encrypted, industry-standard payment gateways. We never sell, rent, or trade your personal details to third-party advertisers.

4. Contact Our Privacy Officer

For questions or requests regarding your personal records, reach out directly at support@sonubookstore.com.
